FAQ

Before you pay a deposit

Booking, payments, visas, cancellations, and what happens if you need help mid-trip.

How do I book a tour?

Choose a country or package, then send the inquiry form with dates and traveler count. We reply with availability and a written outline. A 30% deposit confirms the trip. The balance is due 30 days before departure. You can also request a quote if you want the route changed first.

How do payments work?

Prices on the site are starting rates per person, in US dollars, sharing a double room. We confirm the final figure before you pay. Deposits can be made by card, bank transfer, or mobile money in Ghana. A receipt is sent the same day.

What is usually included?

Each package page lists inclusions. In general you can expect lodging, the guiding named in the itinerary, listed entrance fees, and breakfast. International flights, travel insurance, visas, and most lunches are separate unless the itinerary says otherwise.

Can you help with visas?

We tell you whether your nationality needs a visa, the typical timing, and which documents travelers usually prepare. Embassies make the decision, so we do not guarantee approval. Start visa questions as soon as the destination is chosen.

What is the cancellation policy?

More than 45 days before departure, the deposit is refundable minus any supplier fees already paid. Between 45 and 30 days, the deposit is retained. Inside 30 days, the trip is non-refundable because hotels and parks have been paid. If we cancel a departure, you can move the date or receive a refund of money paid to Passage Tours.

Do I need travel insurance?

Yes. Medical cover, cancellation, and luggage protection should be in place before you pay the balance. We can point you to the kind of policy that fits a safari or a trek, and we ask for the policy number before departure.

Can you design a custom itinerary?

Yes. Use the booking form and choose “Plan a custom itinerary.” Tell us who is traveling, the countries you are weighing, the budget, and anything that must be included — a wedding dinner, a school holiday, a preference for ground-floor rooms.

How large are the groups?

Published packages are private for your party unless the itinerary says “small group.” Iceland’s south-coast days are the usual exception, capped at twelve travelers. Families and couples are the core of the calendar.

What if something goes wrong during the trip?

Save +233 30 123 4567 and the WhatsApp thread before you fly. The Accra desk answers during posted hours, and the after-hours line is for flight changes, medical issues, and lodging problems. Your guide on the ground is the first call; we are the second.

When should I book?

Safari parks, island ferries, and Christmas weeks fill first. Three to six months ahead is comfortable. Inside six weeks we can often still help, with fewer hotel choices. Ghana’s coast is easiest from November to March; ask us before you lock dates for other regions.
