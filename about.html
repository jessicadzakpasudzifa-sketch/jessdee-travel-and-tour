About us

Your next journey starts with someone who knows your name

Jessdee Travel and Tour plans journeys from Accra for people who want the world to feel specific: the right coast, the right pace, a guide who knows the street.

Our story

Planned where the journeys start

The company grew out of a simple frustration: too many trips were either improvised at the airport or locked into a brochure that ignored who was actually traveling. Jessdee Travel and Tour sits on Independence Avenue so the planning conversation can happen in person, on the phone, or on WhatsApp.

Ghana is home ground — Cape Coast, Kakum, the food in Accra — and the same desk arranges safaris, islands, and city routes with partners we have already used. You get one itinerary, one point of contact, and a price that names what is included.

How we work

Trust is a list you can read

Mission

Help people book trips they will still talk about, without making the planning feel like a second job.

Expertise

West African travel first, then a short list of international routes our planners have checked in person or with long-standing guides.

Fit

Individuals, couples, families, and small groups. Tell us the ages and the energy level. The days change accordingly.

Clarity

Inclusions, exclusions, and deposit timing are written before you pay. Questions about visas are answered in plain language.

Care

The same office that designs the route is the number you save. If a flight slips, you are not writing to a call center abroad.

Warmth

Adventure is welcome. So is a slow lunch. Professional does not have to mean hurried.
