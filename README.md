# Jessdee Travel and Tour

A static travel booking website for Jessdee Travel and Tour, planned from Accra. Visitors can explore destinations, compare tour packages, and send a booking or contact inquiry.

**Studio:** 12 Independence Avenue, Accra, Ghana  
**Phone:** +233 30 123 4567  
**Email:** hello@jessdeetravel.com (placeholder — change before launch)  
**WhatsApp:** https://wa.me/233301234567

## Pages

- Home, with destination search
- Explore countries, with region, budget, trip type, and duration filters
- Tour packages, with itineraries, inclusions, and book / quote actions
- Booking inquiry
- About, traveler stories, contact (with map), and FAQ

Inquiries are saved in the browser only. Connect a form service before taking real bookings. Traveler stories on the site are sample copy.
